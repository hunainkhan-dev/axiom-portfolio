About

Hi, I'm Hunain.

I'm a self-taught full-stack developer based in Pakistan, early in my journey and building in public. I design and build websites and web apps with the MERN stack, and the projects on this site are real, working things I've built to sharpen my craft.

I started with a simple obsession: making the web feel more like the curated experiences I grew up loving in film, print, and industrial design. Websites that respect attention, reward curiosity, and hold up to real use.

I'm now looking to take on my first client projects. If you're weighing whether to work together, know this: I don't do beige. I do quiet confidence, deliberate motion, and clean code — and I'll put real care into your project because I'm building my reputation on it.
