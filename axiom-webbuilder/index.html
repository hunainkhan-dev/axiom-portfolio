(01) About

I build things that feel alive.

I'm a self-taught developer just getting started. I build websites and web apps with the MERN stack, and I care a lot about interfaces that feel intentional and animations that respect attention.

I care about the small stuff — the easing curve on a hover, the way a headline lands, the millisecond a form validates. Because that's what separates a website from an experience.

I work with Next.js, React, Node, and MongoDB by default, with GSAP and Three.js when a project asks for craft. But my real toolkit is judgement — knowing what to build, what to cut, and what will still feel fresh in three years.
